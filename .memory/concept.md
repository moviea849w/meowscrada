# Concept — Funny What-Ifs (with facts)

**Status: ACTIVE.** This is the format. Episode 001 (medieval king) is retired as a
one-off; it proved the pipeline but the concept was judged not good.

## The format

A **long-form (16:9, ~5 min) deadpan explainer** that answers an absurd hypothetical
question using real, citable science.

> *"What if you could never sleep?"* → real sleep research, delivered completely
> straight.

The comedy is **the mismatch**: a serious documentary voice narrating something
ridiculous. No winking, no "haha", no exclamation marks. The narrator never admits
the question is silly. That's what makes it funny.

**The rule:** every joke must land on a real fact. If a claim isn't sourced, cut it.

## Why long-form, not shorts

The user's call: *"this won't work for 3D animation. usually works for headphones and
things. guess what, we have to do long form."* The what-if format needs room to build
a timeline and pay it off — 60 seconds can't do that. Long-form also fits the
2-hour/day budget: one 5-minute episode renders in ~10 minutes.

## Language rules

| Track | Language |
|---|---|
| **Narration (audio)** | **Hindi**, no English technical words. Numbers spoken in English. |
| **On-screen titles** | **English only** (no Devanagari in rendered text — font support). |
| **Subtitles** | **Hinglish** — Hindi in Latin script. |

Hinglish subtitles exist because Indian viewers read them faster than Devanagari and
it works for viewers who don't read Hindi script at all. Example:

```
Randy ke saath din 2 pe aisa hua — aankhon ka focus chala gaya.
```

## Tone rules

1. **Deadpan.** Serious voice, absurd subject. Never explain the joke.
2. **Facts carry the humour.** "It is called *fatal* familial insomnia. The name is
   not a metaphor." — the fact does the work.
3. **Build, then deflate.** Set up expectations, then answer honestly and slightly
   worse than expected. "You wouldn't die. You'd just stop being you."
4. **One joke per beat, maximum.** Long-form dies from too many gags.
5. **End on a callback**, not a summary.

## Episode shape (5 min)

| Beat | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Cold open | 0:00–0:15 | State the absurd question. No intro, no logo. |
| The person who tried it | 0:15–1:45 | A real case, day by day. The signature timeline sequence. |
| The mechanism | 1:45–3:30 | What's actually happening inside. The "cleaning crew" idea. |
| The people who couldn't stop | 3:30–4:45 | The disease. The rats. The dark turn. |
| Payoff | 4:45–5:00 | Honest answer + callback to the cold open. |
