# Goal

**Make curiosity-based educational explainer videos for YouTube — long-form and short-form.**

## Format
- **Long-form:** 5–8 min, 16:9 1920x1080, one curiosity question per episode
- **Shorts:** 9:16 1080x1920, 15–60s, cut from the long-form or standalone
- **Style:** open on a question the viewer can't answer, answer it with a story, end on a new question
- **Visuals:** public-domain historic artwork, slow Ken Burns motion, restrained text overlays

## Niche
Curiosity-based educational explainers — history, science, "why is X like that?".
Not quiz/math content (dropped; that was a side experiment).

## Objectives
- Produce long-form episodes consistently
- Cut each long-form into 3–5 shorts/reels
- Build a repeatable pipeline: idea → research → script → art → voice → edit → thumbnail → upload
- Track progress via tickets; log completions in done.md

## Language rules (all videos)
- **Hindi voiceover**, natural everyday Hindi
- **English only on screen** — no Devanagari anywhere in the frame
- **No technical/English technical words in the Hindi audio**
- Numbers spoken in English inside Hindi sentences
- See [voiceover-guide.md](./voiceover-guide.md)

## Success criteria
- [ ] Long-form pipeline set up
- [ ] Episode 1 piloted and approved
- [ ] Episode 1 published
- [ ] 3+ shorts cut from episode 1
- [ ] Regular cadence (e.g. 1 long-form / 2 weeks)
