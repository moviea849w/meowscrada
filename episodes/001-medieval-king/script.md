# Episode 1 — Why Was Being a Medieval King So Dangerous?

**Voice:** Hindi · conversational, curious, light humour · **On screen:** English only
**Angle:** it's not a history lesson about dates. It's about **human nature** — what people do when there's one chair at the top.

## ACT 1 — It was never really about gold

### scene 04 — what a king owned
TEMPLATE: lower-third · ART: castle · KICKER: Act one
TEXT: The real prize wasn't gold
SAY: हम सोचते हैं कि राजा के पास पैसा था। असल में पैसा कागज़ का नहीं था, वह ज़मीन थी। जिसके पास ज़मीन, उसके पास लोग। जिसके पास लोग, उसके पास सेना। और जिसके पास सेना — उसके पास सब कुछ।

### scene 05 — the handshake

### scene 06 — human nature

### scene 07 — one chair

## Production notes
- 20 scenes. Template rotation avoids repeats: no two adjacent scenes share a layout.
- Reused art (richard-portrait, tower, wat-taylor, coronation) always changes `focus` and `motion`.
- Humour is dry and observational, never a joke that breaks the story.
- Every act ends on a question, so the viewer keeps a reason to stay.
- SFX: `bell` on 09, `boom` on 11 and 15, `crowd` under 14–15, `heartbeat` on 17.
- Music: `medieval-ambient` acts 1–2, `drone-tense` act 3, silence on 19.
